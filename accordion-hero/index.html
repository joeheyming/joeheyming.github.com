That's Amore

Notes fall onto your Stradella buttons. Hit them on the strike line — any voicing that fits the chord earns points. Play the suggested combo for the max, or improvise: C6, Cmaj7, even an Am sub all count.

How to play
  • Desktop keys — each Stradella column maps to a vertical slice of four keys (top → bottom on the keyboard = top → bottom on the lane bar):
    • Column 1: 1 Q A Z
    • Column 2: 2 W S X
    • Column 3: 3 E D C
    • … and so on across the keyboard up to column 10 (0 P ; /).
    Within a column, the top key always plays whatever button is at the top of that column on the lane bar — usually the bass note. Each button's key is printed on it.
  • Phone / tablet: tap the column buttons at the bottom. Multi-touch works — hold several at once.
  • Notes fall from the top. Hit them when they cross the pink strike line. White notes are bass; colored notes are chords (green = major, teal = minor, amber = dom7, red = dim7).
  • Any voicing that fits earns points — Perfect (exact or extension, 1.0×), Smooth sub (≥ 3 shared notes, 0.85×), Close (≥ 2 shared, 0.7×), or Miss.
