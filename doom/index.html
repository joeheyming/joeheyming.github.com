Play DOOM online for free in your browser. No install, no plugins, no signup — works unblocked on school and work networks. Pick from eighteen DOOM flavors below: Classic DOOM (the original 1993 id Software shareware), Freedoom Phase 1 (the open-source DOOM-compatible IWAD), the Zelda-themed Legend of DOOM total conversion by DeTwelveGames, Mario DOOM (Valigarmander's MarioDoom gameplay mod layered over Freedoom Phase 1), Super MAYhem (the Super MAYhem 17 Mario map campaign on Freedoom Phase 2, maps fetched at launch), Metroid DOOM (Spram's Metroidvania total conversion with Samus, beam upgrades, energy tanks, and Mother Brain), Castlevania: Simon's Destiny (Batandy's standalone first-person reimagining of the NES Castlevania, with whip combat, sub-weapons, and Castle Dracula across six reimagined levels), and Sonic DOOM (AeriaVelocity's Sonic the Hedgehog in DOOM: Legacy Edition — rings, boost, and Super Sonic over Freedoom Phase 2; from the author of Sonic: Lock & Load), and Batman DOOM (ACE Team's 1999 Doom II total conversion — new weapons, enemies, and maps — fetched from idgames on first launch and loaded on Freedoom Phase 2), and Zombies (T.C.H.G.'s Dawn of the Dead and Day of the Dead campaign — twelve Doom II maps, fetched from idgames on first launch and loaded on Freedoom Phase 2), Sigil and Sigil II (John Romero's free MIDI episodes 5 and 6 on Freedoom Phase 1), and Witching Hour (an eight-map Halloween set on Freedoom Phase 2), DoomCraft (three Minecraft maps), Lego Base (one Lego level), Block Fort (a Mario Kart 64 fort), Roblox DOOM (one joke map), and Pokémon DOOM (John Dizon's Kanto and Johto towns, fetched on first launch). Data & settings

UZDoom is GPL v3. This page ships Freedoom (BSD-style) and the UZDoom WASM engine. Legend of DOOM, Mario DOOM, Metroid DOOM, Castlevania, and Sonic DOOM download their archives when that flavor launches; those files are not in this repo. LegendOfDoom-1.1.0.pk3 is © DeTwelveGames. mariodoom.pk3 is by Valigarmander. Super MAYhem uses that same PK3 and fetches the Super MAYhem 17 map pack from the Doomworld idgames mirror on first launch — the WADs are not in this repo. Batman DOOM fetches the ACE Team zip from that same mirror; the WAD and DeHackEd patch are not in this repo. Zombies fetches T.C.H.G.'s Dawn of the Dead zip from that mirror; those files are not in this repo. Sigil, Sigil II, Witching Hour, DoomCraft, Lego Base, Block Fort, and Roblox DOOM fetch their idgames zips the same way. Pokémon DOOM fetches pkmnv9r.wad (John Dizon's Pokémon DOOM v9r) from the Internet Archive on first launch; that wad is not in this repo. Metroid DOOM fetches met2.pk3 (Spram's Metroid Doom v2.2, bugfix by OkDoomer174). Castlevania fetches Castlevania.ipk3 (Batandy's Castlevania: Simon's Destiny v1.4). Sonic DOOM fetches sonicdoom-legacy.pk3 (AeriaVelocity's Sonic the Hedgehog in DOOM: Legacy Edition, 2023-04-11). Mario, Metroid, and Pokémon assets are © Nintendo, Castlevania is © Konami, and Sonic is © SEGA, used here as non-profit fan projects, not endorsed by those companies.
