    Keyboard (StepMania edit mode) — F1
    1 2 3 4
    Lay a note in that column. Hold Shift for a roll.
    N / M
    Cycle the tap type: tap, mine, lift, fake.
    Space
    Lay an area marker. Press twice to mark a region.
    P
    Play the marked area, or from the cursor. Shift+P from the cursor, Ctrl+P from the start.
    Enter / Escape
    Stop playing and return to editing.
    Up / Down
    Move one snap line. Hold Ctrl to zoom the notefield.
    Left / Right
    Coarser or finer snap.
    PgUp / PgDn, ; / '
    Move four beats.
    , / .
    Jump to the previous or next row that has a note.
    Home / End
    Jump to the start, or to the last note.
    F5 / F6
    Switch to the previous or next chart of the same step type.
    F7 / F8
    BPM at the cursor, down or up.
    F9 / F10
    Stop at the cursor. Hold Shift for a delay.
    F11 / F12
    Music offset.
    [ / ]
    Sample start. Hold Shift for sample length. L previews the sample.
    Alt
    Hold while adjusting for fine steps (a twentieth of the normal amount).
    U, Ctrl+Z
    Undo. Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes.
    T
    Toggle split timing on this chart.
    Ctrl+S
    Save the draft now.
