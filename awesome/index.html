🎬 About This Experience

This is an interactive celebration of the iconic LEGO Movie anthem. While the music plays, enjoy:

  • 🌈 Synchronized lyrics with color-changing backgrounds
  • 🦜 Party parrots and nyan cats flying across the screen
  • 🐱🐕 Random cat & dog photos with fun animal facts
  • 🥰 Adorable images from Reddit's r/aww community
  • 🎆 Fireworks and confetti celebrations
  • 🪩 Disco ball with sweeping lights
  • ✨ Rainbow cursor trails
