    Common questions about local browser LLMs

    Is the model really local? Yes — inference runs on your GPU through WebGPU. Your prompts and any attached files never leave your device.

    Why an 8-billion-parameter model? Hermes-3-Llama-3.1-8B is large enough to be useful for everyday tasks but small enough to fit in 4–6 GB of GPU memory on consumer hardware.

    What are the alternatives if WebGPU isn't available? The page detects unsupported browsers and shows a fallback message. Chrome 113+, Edge 113+, Safari 18+ on macOS, and Firefox 141+ are the working targets.
