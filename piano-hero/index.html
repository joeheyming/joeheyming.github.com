How to play

  • Drop a .mid file anywhere on this page, or click Pick a song to choose from curated pieces or search Internet Archive.
  • Watch mode plays the MIDI through a real-piano sampler so you can follow along.
  • Play-along mode scores your timing. The QWERTY letters drawn on each key show what to press — Z X C V B N M covers the C3 octave, Q W E R T Y U jumps to C4 (middle C), and I O P reaches into C5. Plug in a MIDI controller for full-range playing.
  • Tempo slows things down for practice; the Hands toggles let you mute one hand to learn the other.
