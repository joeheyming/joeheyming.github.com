How to play

  • Tap a string to pluck it, or swipe across them to strum. Multi-touch works — use both hands.
  • Pick a scale and root to retune the strings. Pentatonic scales sound consonant no matter how you strum.
  • The lowest red string of each octave is the root. Strings ring out naturally — there's no damping.
