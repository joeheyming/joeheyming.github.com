How to play

  • Tap a tongue to ring it. The big disc in the middle is the ding (the root, an octave below).
  • Multi-touch friendly — use both hands. Drag from one tongue to another to retrigger them, just like rolling fingers on a real handpan.
  • Try Kurd for a moody minor sound, Akebono for a Japanese pentatonic flavour, or Hijaz for a Middle-Eastern feel.
