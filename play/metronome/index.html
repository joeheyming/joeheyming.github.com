How to use

  • Press Start or hit Space to play. The first beat of each measure is accented.
  • Drag the slider, type a number, or use ↑ / ↓ to change tempo. Hold Shift for ±5 BPM.
  • Tap (or hit T ) at least three times to set the tempo by feel.
  • Try a subdivision like Eighth or Triplet to feel the space between beats.
