Air mode

Wave your hand in front of the camera to play. Hand position sets pitch (left ↔ right) and volume (up ↔ down).

How to play

  • Touch mode — drag on the pad: X sets pitch (left = low, right = high), Y sets volume (bottom = silent, top = loud). A second finger adds vibrato (X = rate, Y = depth).
  • Air mode — wave your hand in front of the front camera. Hand X = pitch, hand Y = volume. With two hands visible: your right hand sets pitch (closer-to-camera = louder like a real theremin antenna), your left hand adds vibrato.
  • Pick a scale to snap pitches to a key — set it to Continuous for true theremin glide.
  • Glide smooths big pitch jumps; turn it down for snappy attacks, up for liquid swoops.
