How to play

  • Click or drag across frets to pluck individual notes.
  • Use the chord builder to pick a Root, Quality, and Shape (open / barre / higher voicings). Press Strum to hear it; the shape stays highlighted on the fretboard so you can study it and play the same notes one at a time. Pink ring marks the root; ◯ = open string; × = muted string.
  • The dots at frets 3, 5, 7, 9 and the double-dot at 12 are the standard guitar inlays.
  • Strings (low to high): E A D G B E. The thicker strings are toward the bottom.
