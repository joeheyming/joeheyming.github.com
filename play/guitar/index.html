How to play

  • Click or drag across frets to pluck individual notes.
  • Use the chord builder to set Quality (Major / m7 / sus4 / …) and Shape (open / barre / higher voicings) — those just configure the chord on the fretboard without playing. Tapping a Root button strums the configured chord, so you can play a song by setting up a quality once and tapping different roots: C → F → G, or set m7 and tap C → A → D. Press Strum to replay the current chord. Pink ring marks the root; ◯ = open string; × = muted string.
  • The dots at frets 3, 5, 7, 9, 15 and 17 plus the double-dot at 12 are the standard guitar inlays. On narrow screens the neck scrolls horizontally — swipe past the 12th fret to reach the upper register.
  • Strings (low to high): E A D G B E. The thicker strings are toward the bottom.
