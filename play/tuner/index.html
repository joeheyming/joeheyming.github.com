Tap a note to play a sustained sine. Tap again to stop. With Clamp on, all references play in the C4–C6 range so you can hear them on a phone speaker.

How to use

  • Pick your Instrument at the top — the string row appears below the note carousel. Tap a string to lock the target ; tap AUTO to snap to the nearest chromatic note instead.
  • Tap Start listening and allow microphone access. Pluck a string and the dial shows how many cents you're sharp (right) or flat (left). The needle goes green inside ±5 ¢ — that's "in tune".
  • The note carousel above the dial shows the current note plus its chromatic neighbours, so you can see which way the next note lies even before the cents settles.
  • A4 defaults to 440 Hz . Drop it to 415 for baroque pitch, or nudge to 442 / 444 for orchestral. Tuning swaps between Equal, Just (centred on C), and Pythagorean — set this for a-cappella or early-music sessions; leave on Equal otherwise.
  • No mic? Open the Reference pitches section and tap any note to play a sustained sine you can ear-tune against.
