How to play

  • Tap a bar to ring it. Drag across the bars to glissando — bars layer naturally, just like real mallet hits.
  • Switch Tone to flip between xylophone (bright wood), marimba (warm wood), vibraphone (sustained metal with optional tremolo), and glockenspiel (high bells).
  • On desktop the QWERTY mapping from the piano works here too: Z–M are the lower-octave naturals, S D G H J are the accidentals, and [ / ] shift the octave.
