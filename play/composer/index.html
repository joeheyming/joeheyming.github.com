How to use

  • Pick a duration ( 1 – 5 ), then click the staff — a preview follows the cursor. Or type A – G at the playhead. R toggles rest mode; 0 places a rest. Click to select. Delete removes a rest, or turns a note into a rest.
  • Drag a note up/down for pitch, left/right for timing. Arrow keys nudge pitch/timing (Shift+←/→ = one 16th). Stack pitches on the same beat for chords. Notes that don’t fit a bar are split and auto-tied into the next. . dots the current duration, T tie adjacent same-pitch notes. Ctrl/Cmd+C / V copy/paste at the playhead. Dynamics ( p – f ) and Voice apply to the selection.
  • Accidentals: # / s sharp, - flat, n natural. Change Time and Key in the toolbar. Use Bars + / − (or hover a bar) to add or remove measures. Pick an Example to load a starter score.
  • Space play/pause; Stop rewinds. Drag the red playhead (or click above/below the staff) to scrub. ⌘/Ctrl+Z undo, ⌘/Ctrl+Shift+Z redo. Scores auto-save here; Export or Copy link to share.
